# Integration: Twilio (WhatsApp & SMS) 📱

Interact with clients and receive proactive alerts directly on your mobile device.

## 🔒 Scopes & Permissions
Configured via Twilio Account SID and Auth Token. Assist Pro acts as a webhook handler for incoming messages and an API client for outgoing ones.

## ⚡ Supported Actions
- `TWILIO_SEND_WHATSAPP`: Dispatch a formatted WhatsApp template or free-form message.
- `TWILIO_SEND_SMS`: Dispatch an SMS message.

## ⚠️ Approval-Sensitive Actions
- Outbound messages to customers (`TWILIO_SEND_WHATSAPP`) generated by the AI require approval in the dashboard to prevent automated hallucination broadcasts.

## 🎣 Webhook Events (Incoming)
- Twilio webhooks can be routed to the Assist Pro orchestrator. This allows you to text your AI assistant ("Hey, push my 3 PM meeting by 15 minutes") and have it execute the Google Calendar changes autonomously.
